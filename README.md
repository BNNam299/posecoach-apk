# PoseCoach — ban cai de test

## Tai ve

**[app-debug.apk](https://github.com/BNNam299/posecoach-apk/raw/main/app-debug.apk)**

Mo link nay bang trinh duyet NGAY TREN DIEN THOAI la tai ve cai duoc.

## Ban nay danh cho DIEN THOAI THAT

File chi chua thu vien cho chip **arm64-v8a** — gan nhu moi dien thoai
Android 10 tro len deu dung chip nay.

**Khong cai duoc len may ao x86 trong Android Studio.** Ban cho may ao
nam o may cua nguoi phat trien (`app-x86_64-debug.apk`), khong day len day
vi khong ai can tai ve.

## Yeu cau may

| Hang muc | Muc toi thieu |
|---|---|
| Con quay hoi chuyen (gyroscope) | BAT BUOC CO |
| Android | 10 (API 29) tro len |
| RAM | 4 GB tro len |

Thieu con quay hoi chuyen thi bon tinh nang huong dan se khong chay dung.

## Luu y

Day la **ban debug** dung de thu nghiem, chua phai ban phat hanh:

- Chua toi uu dung luong (76 MB)
- Dinh danh app van la `com.example.posecoach`, phai doi truoc khi len Google Play
- Cac nguong do dac trong app **chua duoc do tren may that**, huong dan co the
  bao dat/chua dat khong chinh xac
